Request account deletion

Publisher Rigour Consulting Location Perth, Western Australia Effective date 22/11/2026 Direct contact rigour.consulting@gmail.com

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your Consotelo account and associated personal records. This page provides clear instructions on how to initiate an account deletion request from outside the application.

Who can request account deletion

This process is available to all registered users of Consotelo, including independent consultants, practice administrators, and business clients. Any user who has registered an account through an invitation or consultant onboarding may submit a request.

Important distinctions before requesting deletion

Please review these key distinctions before submitting a deletion request:

  • Deleting an account vs uninstalling the app Removing or uninstalling the Consotelo application from your Android phone or Windows desktop does not delete your account or stored data on our servers. You must follow the procedure below to remove your server records.
  • Deleting an account vs concluding a consultation Completing a scheduled advisory session or terminating a client agreement does not close your login account. If you merely wish to end a professional engagement, coordinate with your consultant within the application rather than deleting your account.

How to submit an account deletion request

Because this public website is hosted statically and does not process authenticated transactions, account deletion requests are initiated via our verified administrative support channel:

Step 1. Send your deletion request by email

Using the email address currently registered with your Consotelo account, compose an email to our support team:

  • Recipient rigour.consulting@gmail.com
  • Subject line Request account deletion
  • Body details Include your full name, registered email address, and your Consotelo username.

Security rule: Do not send your account password, payment card numbers, or copies of government identity cards in your email.

Step 2. Identity confirmation

To protect your account from unauthorised deletion, our team will send a verification token to your registered email address within two Australian business days. Reply to that email to confirm your deletion request.

Step 3. Account deactivation and data purging

Once your confirmation is received, your account will be immediately deactivated. Active login credentials, session tokens, multi-factor authentication bindings, and personal profile records will be permanently purged within thirty calendar days. A written confirmation email will be delivered once complete.

What information will be permanently deleted

Upon completion of the deletion process, the following data categories will be permanently destroyed:

  • Login credentials, cryptographic password hashes, and session authentication tokens.
  • Multi-factor authentication enrolment secrets and emergency recovery codes.
  • Profile information, including full name, business display title, and contact preferences.
  • Personal device registrations and local sync metadata.
  • Draft appointment proposals, unacknowledged agreement drafts, and non-essential consultation notes.

Statutory retention exceptions under Australian law

Certain records cannot be deleted immediately upon account closure due to strict statutory compliance requirements under Australian law:

  • Tax invoices and payment receipts Issued Australian tax invoices, payment receipts, fee calculations, and transaction references must be retained for seven years from their creation date pursuant to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 (Cth) and Australian Taxation Office rules.
  • Executed consultation agreements Legally acknowledged contracts, electronic signatures, and cryptographic document digests are retained for seven years from execution to support legal validity and statutory contract dispute periods.

Any records retained under these statutory exceptions are locked into an archived, read-only state. They are strictly segregated from active operational systems and will never be used for marketing or ongoing consulting operations.

Need assistance with your account

If you have questions regarding the deletion process or require assistance, please contact our support desk in Perth, Western Australia at rigour.consulting@gmail.com.
