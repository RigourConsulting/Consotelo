Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for consultants and clients

Plain-English guidance on Australian GST handling, timing, agreements, privacy, SMS, and current internal-testing status.

Does every Australian consultant have to charge GST in Consotelo?

No. Rigour Consulting is not currently registered for Australian GST, so Consotelo issues ordinary invoices stating 'No GST has been charged (not registered for GST)' and does not label them as a Tax Invoice. Separately configured GST-registered practices can issue compliant Tax Invoices with GST-exclusive, GST-inclusive and GST-free items. Non-GST registration remains distinct from income-tax obligations.

Does finishing a consultation timer automatically send an invoice?

No. Finishing a consultation records the verified duration and fee calculation only. The consultant always reviews and authorises invoice creation, issuing, payment receipts and overdue reminders.

What happens if I accidentally start and stop the timer after 6 seconds?

Under exact-minute and nearest-30-minute rounding with no minimum billable duration configured, a 6-second session rounds to 0 billable minutes (A$0.00). Your configured rounding rule and any agreed minimum duration are displayed clearly on screen before you finalise.

Can clients print an agreement and sign it by hand instead of signing electronically?

Yes. Both consultants and clients can download and print a formatted A4 PDF with clearly labelled handwritten signature blocks, sign on paper, and upload a scanned or photographed copy (PDF, JPG or PNG). Uploaded copies require manual consultant review before finalisation. Drawn, uploaded-image and typed-name electronic acknowledgements are also supported.

Is an electronic acknowledgement in Consotelo a certificate-based digital signature?

No. Consotelo records explicit signing consent, account identity, timestamps and SHA-256 document revision hashes, but does not represent typed, drawn or uploaded signatures as certificate-based digital signatures (PKI) and does not certify legal enforceability without independent Australian legal review.

Can clients create invoices, change fees or view other clients' records?

No. Role permissions and practice tenant boundaries are enforced on the server for every API request. Clients can only view, download and respond to records belonging to their own authorised relationships.

How do partial payments and overdue reminders work?

Verified partial payments reduce the invoice's live outstanding balance immediately. First reminders, second reminders and final notices always reference the single original invoice without creating a new charge, and overdue notices are automatically suppressed once an invoice is paid in full.

How does the Android 'Send SMS reminder' feature work, and does it cost money?

Tapping 'Send SMS reminder' opens your phone's default Messages app with a prefilled reminder draft so you can review, edit or cancel it before sending under your ordinary mobile plan. Consotelo does not request restricted SMS permissions and never sends paid automated SMS messages by default.

Are QR payment links enabled on every invoice?

Only when a practice configures a permanent, verified HTTPS payment domain. Consotelo blocks local development addresses, placeholder domains and temporary testing tunnels from appearing in QR codes while preserving configured bank-transfer instructions.

What authentication and two-step verification options are supported?

Consotelo supports password sign-in, optional RFC 6238 authenticator-app two-step verification (6-digit codes) and single-use recovery codes. Standards-based passkeys and Windows Hello are built in and activate once a practice connects to a permanent HTTPS domain; rotating temporary test tunnels intentionally disable passkeys.

Can I download Consotelo from Google Play or sign up for a live cloud account today?

Not yet. Consotelo v1.0.0 is currently prepared for controlled internal testing on Android, Windows desktop and web browsers. Permanent commercial hosting and public store release are not yet live.
