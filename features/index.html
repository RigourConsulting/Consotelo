Features & platforms

Everything needed to run structured consultations

Each capability below states plainly what works in current internal testing and what remains optional or disabled until configured by a practice owner.

Scheduling & time zones

Propose consultations from either the consultant or client account, review counter-proposals, cancel with clear status history, and export standard calendar .ics files without exposing private notes or fees.

All schedules present cleanly in the practice's local time zone (defaulting to Australia/Perth) and automatically move elapsed sessions out of Upcoming lists.

Available in internal testing

Consultation timer & charging rules

Start, pause, resume and stop consultation timers with resilient local persistence when connectivity drops briefly. Configured rounding increments (exact minute, nearest 30 minutes, or round up to next 30 minutes) and any minimum charge are shown before finalising.

Stopping or finishing a session records duration only — consultants explicitly decide when to draft and issue an invoice.

Available in internal testing

Australian non-GST & GST invoicing

Supports multi-line invoices with hourly, unit, or fixed-fee items, precise Australian-dollar integer-cent arithmetic, partial payments, verified payment receipts, statements of account, and non-duplicating overdue reminders.

Practices not registered for GST (including Rigour Consulting) issue ordinary INVOICE PDFs stating No GST has been charged. Separately configured GST-registered practices issue compliant TAX INVOICE documents supporting GST-exclusive, GST-inclusive, and GST-free lines. Historical issued snapshots are locked against retrospective profile changes.

Available in internal testing

Flexible agreements & signing

Six agreement types and six payment arrangements (before commencement, at commencement, scheduled milestones, agreed dates, deposit plus balance, and after completion), custom consultant sections, and client-proposed revisions.

Download and print A4 PDFs for handwritten signature and upload (PDF, JPG or PNG with manual consultant review), or use drawn, uploaded-image, or typed-name electronic acknowledgement. Finalised originals remain immutable; subsequent changes create a new auditable amendment.

Available in internal testing

Notifications & manual SMS composer

In-app notifications alert users to booking updates, document actions and reminders. On Android, consultants can open their device's own Messages app with a prefilled reminder draft via Send SMS reminder — Consotelo never requests restricted SMS permissions or sends paid SMS automatically.

An optional provider-neutral automated SMS adapter (with ACMA opt-out and monthly budget caps) stays disabled by default.

Manual SMS active • Automated SMS disabled

Payments & QR links

Manual EFT bank-transfer instructions and consultant-verified payment reconciliation work out of the box. Public QR payment links activate only when a practice configures a permanent verified HTTPS payment domain (local development addresses and temporary tunnels are blocked).

Card, PayPal and automated PayID settlements remain disabled until a practice connects an authorised payment provider.

Bank transfer active • Online gateways optional
