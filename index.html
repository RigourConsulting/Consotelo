Professional consultation software

One place for every consultation

Consotelo brings clarity to professional consulting relationships. It coordinates appointment scheduling, tracks consultation duration with monotonic precision, generates Australian tax invoices, and maintains verified agreement records.

Designed for independent consultants and their clients

Professional advisory work requires clear operational boundaries. Consotelo replaces disconnected spreadsheets, messaging chains, and manual time calculations with an integrated system built around mutual consent.

For consultants and advisors

Coordinate appointments with clients across different time zones. Run an accurate session timer during consultations with clear start, pause, resume, and stop controls.

Generate formal Australian tax invoices in integer cents with accurate Goods and Services Tax calculations. Issue signed payment receipts only after funds are verified.

For business clients

Review and negotiate appointment proposals before committing your schedule. Access an agenda synchronised to your local business time zone.

Review consultation agreements in full detail before providing electronic acknowledgement. Access historical tax invoices and payment receipts whenever you need them.

Core capabilities

Two-way scheduling

Either party can propose an appointment time. Appointments become confirmed only when the counterparty accepts, preventing scheduling surprises and double bookings.

Session time tracking

A dedicated monotonic session timer records billable work. Stopping the timer updates the internal session ledger while preserving consultant control over final billing.

Australian tax invoices

Invoices include validated eleven-digit Australian Business Numbers, itemised hourly rates, net fees, ten percent Goods and Services Tax, and unique sequential numbering.

Six payment settlement methods

Support for PayID, direct bank transfer, card payments, PayPal, cash, and card-present transactions. Invoices transition to paid status only after verified receipt.

Printable agreements

Consultation agreements are rendered into complete A4 documents. Cryptographic byte digests ensure that the document signed matches the exact revision drafted.

Tenant isolation

Every practice operates within a protected business boundary. Client records, appointment notes, agreements, and financial figures remain isolated and confidential.

Release status and platform availability
