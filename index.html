Designed for independent consultants and their clients

Professional advisory work requires clear operational boundaries. Consotelo replaces disconnected spreadsheets, messaging chains, and manual time calculations with an integrated system built around mutual consent.

For consultants and advisors

Coordinate appointments with clients across time zones (including recurring schedules and RFC 5545 .ics export and previewed import). Run an offline-resilient monotonic session timer with clear start, pause, resume, and stop controls.

Manage service catalogues, client quotations (strictly distinct from tax invoices), business expenses with receipt attachments, custom practice branding across Classic, Modern, and Corporate A4 templates, normalised multi-phone and purpose-routed email contacts, Australian non-GST ordinary invoices or GST tax invoices, GST turnover monitoring, and private income-tax planning reserves. Issue payment receipts only after funds are verified.

For business clients

Review and negotiate appointment proposals before committing your schedule. Access an agenda synchronised to your local business time zone.

Review consultation agreements and quotations in full detail before providing electronic acknowledgement or acceptance. Access historical invoices and payment receipts, manage multiple contact details, receive in-app notifications, and share optional private satisfaction feedback after completed consultations.

Core capabilities

Two-way scheduling

Either party can propose an appointment time. Appointments become confirmed only when the counterparty accepts, preventing scheduling surprises and double bookings.

Session time tracking

An accurate session timer records billable work. Stopping the timer updates session records while preserving consultant control over final billing.

Australian invoicing & tax planning

Supports both non-GST ordinary invoices and GST-registered tax invoices (exclusive, inclusive, GST-free, and input-taxed items), consultant-private income-tax reserves, PAYG instalment tracking, and GST turnover monitoring. Planning summaries are advisory aids only and do not constitute tax advice or automatic ATO lodgement.

Payment tracking

Support for direct bank transfer, PayID, card payments, PayPal, cash, and card-present transactions. Invoices transition to paid status only after verified receipt.

Printable agreements

Consultation agreements are rendered into complete A4 documents. Cryptographic byte digests ensure that the document signed matches the exact revision drafted.

Tenant isolation

Every practice operates within a protected business boundary. Client records, appointment notes, agreements, and financial figures remain isolated and confidential.

Release status and platform availability

Consotelo is engineered for Android, Windows desktop, and web browsers, and is currently prepared for controlled internal testing and local Windows desktop installation.
