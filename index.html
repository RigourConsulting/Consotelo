Structured workflow

How a consultation flows from start to receipt

Every stage keeps clear boundaries between consultant authority and client consent.

  1. 1

    Agree scope & payment terms

    Draft terms, review client proposals, and sign electronically or print to sign by hand.

  2. 2

    Confirm the appointment together

    Either party proposes a time; bookings confirm only after both consultant and client accept.

  3. 3

    Track the consultation

    Start, pause, resume and review elapsed time with visible rounding rules before finalising.

  4. 4

    Review & issue the invoice

    Consultants create and approve every invoice manually — finishing a session never bills automatically.

  5. 5

    Pay & record a receipt

    Partial and full bank transfers update live balances; receipts issue only once payment is verified.

Flexible in practice: Engagements do not need to follow a rigid straight line — agreements can be drafted before an initial booking or amended cleanly as consulting scope evolves.

Two-way bookings with local time zones

Both consultants and clients can propose times, decline, or suggest alternatives without losing track of the thread.

  • Appointments confirm only when the other party accepts.
  • Past consultations move automatically out of Upcoming views.
  • Export clean calendar files (.ics) without exposing private notes or fees.

Reviewable consultation time

Track active consulting duration clearly even when mobile reception drops temporarily, then review before finalising.

  • Configured rounding rules and any minimum duration are shown before finalising.
  • Brief accidental starts (such as 6 seconds) do not create unexplained charges.
  • Stopping or finishing a session never creates an automatic invoice.

Built for both non-GST and GST-registered Australian practices

Not every independent Australian consultant is registered for GST. Consotelo handles both configurations accurately and locks issued snapshots permanently.

  • Not GST-registered: Issues an ordinary INVOICE stating No GST has been charged.
  • GST-registered: Issues a compliant TAX INVOICE supporting GST-exclusive, GST-inclusive and GST-free items.
  • Partial payments, receipts, statements and overdue reminders always reference one original invoice.

Print-and-sign by hand or sign electronically

Choose from six consulting agreement types and six payment structures, negotiate terms with clients, and keep finalised revisions locked.

  • Download and print A4 PDFs with handwritten signature blocks, then upload scanned or photographed signed copies.
  • Or draw a signature, upload a signature image, or type a name with explicit consent (distinct from certificate-based digital signatures).
  • Subsequent scope changes create a new auditable amendment draft without altering the finalised original.
