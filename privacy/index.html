Privacy policy

Publisher Rigour Consulting Location Perth, Western Australia Effective date 25/11/2026 Contact rigour.consulting@gmail.com

This privacy policy explains how Consotelo and Rigour Consulting handle personal information across our applications, web interfaces, and public information pages. We manage personal data in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

About Consotelo and this policy

Consotelo is a professional consultation management platform operated by Rigour Consulting, based in Perth, Western Australia. Consotelo enables independent consultants, advisors, and their clients to schedule appointments, track consultation sessions, manage consultation agreements, and generate Australian invoices (ordinary invoices when not registered for GST and tax invoices when registered for GST).

This policy describes the categories of information we collect, the lawful purposes for which that information is handled, how records are protected, and the procedures available to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data.

Distinction between application services and this static website

It is helpful to distinguish between the Consotelo software application and this public website:

  • Consotelo software application Handles account authentication, appointment schedules, session timer logs, agreement reviews, and tax invoices. Data submitted within the application is processed securely on dedicated database services.
  • Public information website Hosted statically using GitHub Pages. This public website does not use cookies, does not collect personal customer information, does not host login forms, and does not process payments. Standard server transit logs containing IP addresses may be processed by GitHub, Inc. solely to serve static assets and ensure network security.

Personal information collected by the application

We collect only the information necessary to provide consultation coordination, billing, and document administration. Depending on your account role, this includes:

  • Account identification and governance Full name, professional title, business name, primary authentication email address, salted password hashes, active session token hashes, role assignments, and administrative lifecycle/audit logs.
  • Multiple contact details Optional additional telephone numbers (Australian and international) and purpose-routed email addresses (general, booking, billing, accounts) stored separately from authentication and account recovery credentials.
  • Practice branding assets Optional business logos, authorised signatory images, and uploaded letterheads (sanitised with metadata stripped and PDF letterheads safely rasterised into static images) scoped strictly to the uploading practice.
  • Scheduling, calendar imports and exports Proposed and confirmed consultation slots, recurring schedules, participant names, business time zones, shared notes, RFC 5545 .ics calendar exports (excluding private notes and fees), and consultant-private imported external busy periods.
  • Session duration records Start instants, end instants, active elapsed minutes, pause segments, offline-resilient local sync queues, and consultant internal notes.
  • Financial, quotation, expense and invoicing records Australian Business Numbers (where provided), service catalogue definitions, client quotations and acceptance snapshots, business expenses and optional receipt attachments (PDF/JPEG/PNG), GST classifications where applicable, sequential invoice numbers, payment receipts, and strictly consultant-private income-tax planning reserves and manual PAYG instalment records (never disclosed to clients).
  • Consultation agreements and optional client feedback Agreement titles, terms, agreed fee snapshots, PDF cryptographic digests, electronic acknowledgement records, and optional post-consultation satisfaction ratings and written comments shared privately with the practice only.

Purposes of data processing

We collect and handle personal information for the following direct business purposes:

  • Creating and managing authenticated user accounts for consultants and clients.
  • Facilitating two-way scheduling and calendar coordination between consulting parties.
  • Calculating consultation fees and recording verified payment settlements across authorised payment methods.
  • Generating Australian ordinary invoices or GST tax invoices and payment receipts (where authorised by the consultant).
  • Verifying the cryptographic integrity of consultation agreements prior to electronic acknowledgement.
  • Maintaining an immutable audit history of commercial agreements and billing events to resolve disputes.
  • Meeting statutory tax, accounting, and corporate obligations under Australian law.

Data sharing and disclosure boundaries

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to advertisers or marketing aggregators. Information is shared strictly within defined operational boundaries:

  • Between consulting parties Appointment details, agreements, invoices, and payment receipts are shared exclusively between the consultant and client within their specific business tenant.
  • Payment settlement providers When an online payment is processed, transaction details are communicated to the relevant authorised payment processor (such as Stripe or PayPal) to complete settlement. Raw credit card numbers and security codes remain with the payment processor and are never stored within Consotelo.
  • Statutory requirements Personal information may be disclosed where required by an Australian law, court order, or official request from a regulatory authority such as the Australian Taxation Office.

Overseas data disclosure

Consotelo is designed and operated from Western Australia. Core application database services and primary records are hosted within Australian cloud regions where feasible. Certain technical infrastructure components, such as GitHub Pages static hosting or global payment gateways, may process encrypted transit data in international facilities. Where cross-border processing occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure that recipient service providers maintain security standards comparable to the Australian Privacy Principles.

Data retention and statutory storage rules

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil operational consulting services and comply with Australian statutory obligations:

  • Active account data User profiles, proposed appointments, and active consultation drafts are maintained while the account remains active.
  • Financial and tax records Issued tax invoices, payment receipts, fee calculations, and transaction references are retained for seven years from the date of issue to comply with Australian Taxation Office requirements.
  • Executed agreements Acknowledged consultation agreements and their cryptographic hash records are retained for seven years to support legal validity and contract dispute resolution.

Data security measures

We implement multiple technical and procedural controls to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss:

  • Authentication tokens are stored as irreversible cryptographic SHA-256 hashes with session expiry and revocation support.
  • Passwords are stored using strong salted cryptographic hashing, protected by login rate limiting, recent password confirmation for privileged actions, and secure recovery separated from ordinary contact emails.
  • Multi-tenant architecture enforces complete data isolation between consulting practices at the database query level.
  • All remote network transmissions use HTTPS / Transport Layer Security, and privileged platform administration is restricted to trusted local environments during internal testing.
  • Issued financial documents and agreements capture immutable snapshots and SHA-256 byte digests so historical records are never silently altered.

Your rights of access and correction

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to request access to the personal information held about you and to request corrections if that information is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, or misleading. You may review and update your profile details directly within the application, or submit a formal access request by contacting our support team at rigour.consulting@gmail.com.

Account deletion procedure

You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. For full instructions on how to initiate this process from outside the application, please review our dedicated account deletion page. Where statutory obligations require us to retain specific tax invoices or legal agreements, those records will be securely archived and removed from active system access.
