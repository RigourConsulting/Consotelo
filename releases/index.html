An honest, up-to-date view of what is built and verified locally versus what requires owner infrastructure or Google Play Console approval.

Key changes in the v1.0.0 internal-testing candidate (26/11/2026)

  • Complete agreement download, A4 print, handwritten scan upload with manual consultant review, electronic acknowledgement options, six agreement types, six payment structures, and auditable amendments.
  • Australian non-GST ordinary invoicing (default for Rigour Consulting) alongside full GST-registered tax-invoice calculation and immutable issued snapshots.
  • Authenticator-app two-step verification (6-digit numeric entry) separated from one-time recovery codes, plus passkey/Windows Hello gating on temporary tunnels.
  • Native Android Send SMS reminder system-composer integration with complete prefilled reminder text and zero restricted SMS permissions; optional automated SMS provider kept disabled by default.
  • Redesigned public information website with product tour, workflow guide, FAQ, updated Security page, structured metadata and accessible navigation.
