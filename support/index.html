Common support topics

Account access and authentication

Invitations and onboarding Client accounts are invitation-led. When a consultant invites you to collaborate, you receive an invitation with an activation link to set your secure password.

Multi-factor authentication If you lose access to your authenticator application, contact support from your registered email address with your account username to initiate identity verification and recovery.

Appointment scheduling and time zones

Two-way negotiation Appointments remain in proposed status until accepted by the other party. Either party can decline or suggest an alternative time slot.

Time zone coordination Consotelo coordinates booking times using Universal Coordinated Time instants while displaying schedules in your designated business time zone, default Australia/Perth.

Session timing and ledger records

Timer operation The monotonic timer measures active consultation work. If you need a break, use the pause button to ensure inactive time is excluded from billable duration.

Consultant governance Stopping a session timer records elapsed work in the internal session ledger. Stopping a timer does not automatically bill a client or issue an invoice.

Tax invoices and payment receipts

Invoice generation Consultants author tax invoices manually based on agreed rates and session duration. Invoices include verified Australian Business Numbers and itemised Goods and Services Tax.

Receipt issuance Payment receipts are issued separately after payment verification across any of our six supported payment methods.

Consultation agreements and PDF review

Document integrity Every agreement PDF is accompanied by a cryptographic SHA-256 digest to ensure that the document presented matches the draft approved by the consultant.

Electronic acknowledgement Acknowledging an agreement requires reviewing the full terms, ticking legal consent, and entering your typed full name signature mark.

Privacy inquiries and data deletion

Reviewing your data You can request a summary of your personal information held in Consotelo by emailing support.

Account deletion To permanently close your account and delete your records, review the detailed instructions on our account deletion page.

Support escalation process

If your inquiry involves a disputed fee calculation, an unresolved billing discrepancy, or a suspected privacy incident, please specify "Urgent Operational Review" in your email subject line. Our senior technical and privacy officers will review the matter within twenty-four hours during Western Australian business days.
