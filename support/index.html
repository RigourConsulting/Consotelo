Account access and authentication

Invitations and onboarding Client accounts are invitation-led. When a consultant invites you to collaborate, you receive an invitation with an activation link to set your secure password.

Account recovery and security Password recovery operates exclusively through your verified primary authentication email address; secondary billing or booking contact emails cannot be used for login or recovery. If you lose access to your account, contact support from your registered email address with your account username.
