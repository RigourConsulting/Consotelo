Introduction and operational scope

Consotelo is a software platform designed to facilitate scheduling, session timing, agreement drafting, and tax invoicing between independent consultants and their clients. Consultants using Consotelo operate as independent business entities and are solely responsible for their own professional advice, licensing, insurance, statutory compliance, and contractual commitments.

Separation of platform terms from consulting agreements

  • Consultant and client agreements The commercial terms, advisory scope, deliverables, and professional fees agreed between a consultant and a client are governed by their own specific consultation agreement drafted within the platform. Consotelo is not a party to those bilateral professional contracts.

Appointment scheduling and billing rules

The platform facilitates commercial interactions under the following operating rules:

  • Two-way confirmation An appointment proposal does not constitute a confirmed booking until accepted by the counterparty within the application.
  • Monotonic timing The consultation timer provides a measurement of elapsed session duration. Final fee determination and invoice generation remain under the direct human authorisation of the consultant.
  • Currency and tax invoicing All fee amounts are recorded and calculated in Australian Dollars using integer cents. Invoices issued within Australia include ten percent Goods and Services Tax where the issuing consultant has configured valid tax status.
  • Payment settlement Recording a settlement or issuing a receipt requires independent verification by the consultant across one of our six supported settlement methods (PayID, direct bank transfer, card payments, PayPal, cash, or card present). Consotelo does not automatically withdraw funds from your bank account without separate provider authorisation.

Electronic acknowledgement and agreement disclaimers

Consotelo allows consultants to draft consultation agreements and present them for client review and electronic acknowledgement. By ticking the consent checkbox and typing your full name mark, you record an auditable electronic acknowledgement bound to the specific SHA-256 byte digest of the PDF document.

Electronic acknowledgement provides an auditable record of agreement under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth). It is not a certificate-based digital signature. The legal suitability of electronic acknowledgement depends on the specific commercial context and applicable statutory rules governing your jurisdiction.
